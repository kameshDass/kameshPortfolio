Featured project

Automating the escalation grind

A workflow built to remove the manual steps between "customer feedback" and "the right team acting on it."

HQI Smart Feedback Escalation System

Previously, agents manually reviewed customer feedback, wrote escalation notes, identified the right team and submitted requests one by one — slowing every resolution down. This system replaces that with a one-click Schedule workflow that generates standardised escalation notes and routes the request automatically.

Problem

  • Feedback had to be manually reviewed before escalation.
  • Agents wrote escalation notes for every single request.
  • Identifying the right team consumed extra time.
  • Manual handling delayed customer issue resolution.
  • Repetitive operational work reduced overall productivity.

Solution

  • Agents validate customer feedback before escalation.
  • One click on "Schedule" initiates the workflow.
  • Standardised escalation notes generate automatically.
  • The request routes instantly to the right team.
  • The responsible team can act immediately, with no manual handoff.

Business impact

Reduced manual escalation effort
Faster customer issue resolution
Higher SLA compliance
Standardised notes across agents
Better cross-team collaboration
Higher overall operational productivity
